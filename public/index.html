How to Play — Cambio

54 cards (52 + 2 Jokers). Each player gets 4 cards face down in a square. Peek at your 2 nearest cards, put them back, and remember them. Lowest total wins.

  • Your turn: draw from the face-down pile, or take the top card of the discard pile. A discard-pile card must be swapped in — everyone saw it, so it can be snapped. A drawn card may replace one of yours (the replaced card goes face-up on the discard pile) or be put straight on the discard pile without swapping.
  • Discard pile: starts empty. The first face-up card is one of player one's starting four.
  • Snap: any player may snap one of their own or an opponent's cards onto the face-up discard if it is the same rank. Snapping your own leaves you with fewer cards. Snapping an opponent's lets you give them one of your cards. Failed snap: the card goes back, and you draw a face-down penalty card you cannot look at.
  • Powers — mandatory: when you discard a Jack, Queen or King you MUST use its power, after about 4 seconds for snaps. King: look at any one card and swap any two cards. Queen: look at any one card. Jack: swap two cards.
  • Values: Joker 0, Ace 1, numbers face value, Jacks, Queens and black Kings 10, red King −1.
  • Cambio: on your turn, call Cambio when you are happy with your score. Your cards become immune — they cannot be swapped, looked at or snapped. Everyone else gets one more turn, then all cards turn over and lowest wins.
  • Tie-break: on a tie, the player with the most cards wins. If card counts are equal, the player who did not call Cambio wins.
  • Going first: before dealing, each player picks a random card from a fanned-out face-down deck. Lowest Cambio value wins and goes first (red King −1 best, J/Q/black King 10 worst). Tied players redraw.
  • Card memory: in multiplayer the host decides on/off and it is the same for everyone. Solo vs the computer, you choose.
  • AI: Easy is casual and forgetful with slow snaps; Moderate is smarter and quicker; Hard has perfect memory of every card it has seen and snaps very fast.
